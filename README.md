# Global Ads Performance Dashboard Project

## Overview

I am excited to present the Global Ads Performance Analysis Dashboard, an advanced Excel analytics project designed to transform raw digital advertising campaign data into actionable business intelligence. This project provides an interactive reporting solution that enables marketers and business stakeholders to evaluate advertising performance across multiple campaigns, channels, audiences, and regions. By combining advanced Excel analytics with dynamic dashboards, the project helps optimize advertising strategies, maximize return on investment (ROI), and improve campaign effectiveness through data-driven decision-making.

## Problem Statement

The primary objective of this project was to develop a comprehensive monitoring solution for evaluating global digital advertising performance. The available dataset consisted of campaign-level advertising metrics including impressions, clicks, conversions, advertising spend, revenue, CTR, CPC, CPA, and ROI. Analyzing campaign effectiveness across different marketing channels and audience segments was difficult using raw data alone. Therefore, the project focused on building an interactive Excel dashboard capable of simplifying performance analysis through dynamic filtering and meaningful visualizations.

## Analysis Done

The file in which the analysis is done is Global Ads Performance Dashboard.xlsx

A comprehensive data analysis and dashboard development approach was implemented to ensure accurate reporting and meaningful business insights. The analytical workflow included:

- **Data Cleaning**: Performed data preprocessing by handling missing values, removing duplicate records, correcting inconsistent data formats, and validating advertising metrics.
  
- **Data Modeling**: Structured the advertising dataset into organized tables suitable for Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and dashboard reporting.
  
- **KPI Development**: Created business KPIs including **Total Impressions**, **Total Clicks**, **Total Conversions**, **Total Ad Spend**, **Revenue Generated**, **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**, **Cost Per Click (CPC)**, **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**, and **Return on Investment (ROI)**.
  
- **Campaign Performance Analysis**: Evaluated advertising campaign performance across different marketing channels to identify high-performing and underperforming campaigns.
  
- **Audience Segmentation**: Analyzed campaign effectiveness across customer demographics, audience segments, devices, and geographic locations to identify the most profitable target audiences.
  
- **Statistical Analysis**: Conducted correlation and regression analysis to understand relationships between advertising spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and revenue generation.
  
-**Interactive Dashboard Development**: Built a dynamic Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to enable real-time business exploration and performance monitoring.

## Dashboard

The final deliverable consists of an interactive Excel dashboard that provides a comprehensive overview of global advertising campaign performance through dynamic visualizations and cross-filtering capabilities.

## Dashboard Features

- **Campaign Performance Overview**: Displays key performance indicators including **Total Campaigns**, **Total Impressions**, **Total Clicks**, **Total Conversions**, **Total Revenue**, **Total Advertising Spend**, and **Overall ROI**.
 
- **Advertising Channel Analysis**: Compares campaign performance across multiple advertising channels to identify the most effective marketing platforms.
  
- **Revenue & ROI Analysis**: Evaluates campaign profitability by comparing advertising spend with generated revenue, enabling better investment decisions.
  
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR) Analysis**: Measures audience engagement by analyzing click-through rates across campaigns and marketing channels.
  
- **Conversion Performance Analysis**: Identifies campaigns with the highest conversion rates, supporting optimization of advertising strategies.
  
- **Cost Analysis**: Examines Cost Per Click (CPC) and Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) to evaluate campaign efficiency and budget utilization.
  
- **Audience & Geographic Analysis**: Analyzes campaign performance across customer segments, locations, and devices to identify the most responsive audiences.
  
- **Interactive Filters**: Enables users to dynamically filter dashboard insights using campaign names, advertising channels, regions, audience types, devices, campaign dates, and other business dimensions.

## Recommendations

Based on the insights generated from the dashboard, the following strategic recommendations are proposed:

- **Increase Investment in High-ROI Campaigns**: Allocate a larger share of the advertising budget to campaigns consistently delivering the highest return on investment.
  
- **Optimize Low-Performing Campaigns**: Review campaigns with low CTR, poor conversion rates, or high acquisition costs to improve creative content, audience targeting, and bidding strategies.
  
- **Strengthen Audience Targeting**: Focus marketing efforts on customer segments and geographic regions demonstrating higher engagement and conversion performance.
  
- **Improve Budget Allocation**: Continuously monitor advertising spend across channels to maximize campaign efficiency while minimizing unnecessary marketing expenses.
  
- **Reduce Customer Acquisition Cost**: Optimize bidding strategies and campaign targeting to lower Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) without compromising conversion volume.
  
- **Monitor Campaign Performance Regularly**: Track campaign KPIs continuously to identify performance fluctuations early and implement timely optimization strategies.
  
- **Leverage Data-Driven Marketing Decisions**: Utilize dashboard insights to refine advertising strategies, improve customer engagement, and maximize overall marketing effectiveness.
